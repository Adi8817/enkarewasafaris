Frequently Asked Questions About Kenya & Tanzania Safaris from the USA

Answers to common questions from US travellers planning a safari in Kenya and Tanzania.

The cost depends on your destination, travel dates, number of days, accommodation and safari style. Enkarewa Safaris offers mid-range and luxury safari experiences in Kenya and Tanzania, and our team can prepare a personalized quotation based on your preferences and budget. International flights from the USA are generally separate from the safari package unless otherwise agreed.

June through October is a popular period for wildlife viewing, with relatively dry conditions in many safari destinations. January and February can also offer excellent wildlife experiences. The best time depends on your preferred destinations, wildlife interests and whether you want to experience the Great Migration.

Yes. Kenya and Tanzania are excellent destinations for encountering the Big Five: lions, elephants, leopards, buffalo and rhinos. However, wildlife sightings are never guaranteed. Your itinerary can be designed around destinations known for opportunities to encounter these animals.

The Great Migration can be experienced in both Kenya and Tanzania, depending on the season. It moves through the Serengeti ecosystem in Tanzania and into Kenya's Maasai Mara. We can recommend an itinerary based on the expected migration location during your travel dates.

For a first-time visitor travelling from the USA, approximately 7–12 days can provide a good opportunity to explore multiple wildlife destinations without making the itinerary excessively rushed. Shorter 4-day safaris are also available for travelers focusing on a single destination.
